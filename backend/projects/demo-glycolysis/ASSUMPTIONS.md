# Assumptions — Demo: Glycolysis Segment

Units: concentration = mM, time = s

- **[boundary]** ATP and ADP are treated as buffered boundary metabolites (fixed pools).
- **[approximation]** This is a didactic reduced chain, not a genome-scale or full EMP pathway.
- **[units]** Concentrations in mM; time in arbitrary seconds suitable for classroom demos.
- **[kinetics]** PFK inhibition modeled as competitive Michaelis–Menten vs citrate.
- **[compartment]** Well-mixed single cytosolic compartment; no transport delays.
